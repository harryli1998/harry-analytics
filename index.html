A FEW THINGS TO KNOW
Do I need to share access to my store?

No store login is required for an initial conversation. If a pilot makes sense, we can discuss a minimal order export with pseudonymous customer IDs, appropriate permissions and an agreed transfer method.

Can you guarantee more sales?

No. Analysis can reveal patterns and inform decisions; it does not establish that a proposed campaign will cause growth. Incremental effects require a suitable experiment.

Will you publish my business data?

Client names, data, screenshots and results would only be published with separate explicit permission. The GitHub example uses a public historical dataset.

What does this website collect?

This static website has no analytics trackers, account system or upload form. The email button opens your email app and sends nothing automatically. Hosting services may process standard access logs. Don’t email sensitive data before agreeing on handling and retention.
